Identifying music

Everything is looked up in MusicBrainz, then Discogs, then Bandcamp, and filed under the first confident match. Anything none of them is sure of goes to Review.

Discogs answers without one, at 25 lookups a minute. With a token from discogs.com → Settings → Developers it allows 60.

Scrobbling

Cosine sends every account's plays to Last.fm or ListenBrainz, whichever app played them, once the account connects in Sine (Settings → Scrobbling). ListenBrainz needs nothing here.

For Last.fm, make an API account once at last.fm/api/account/create (any name; the callback URL can be left empty) and paste its key and secret here.
